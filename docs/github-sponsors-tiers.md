# GitHub Sponsors - Tier-Texte zum Einfügen

> GitHub Sponsors hat keine API für Tiers. Einmalig manuell: github.com/sponsors → "Join the waitlist" / "Set up GitHub Sponsors" für den Account Sorcecoder → Payout einrichten (Stripe) → dann unter "Sponsor tiers" die drei Tiers anlegen und die Texte unten einfügen. Der Sponsor-Button im Repo (FUNDING.yml) wird aktiv, sobald das Listing live ist.

---

## Tier 1: Silver - $100 / month

**Name:** 🥈 Silver Sponsor

**Description:**

You keep the eval gate running. Every jerrycan release passes an agent-driven conformance eval on real infrastructure, and that infrastructure costs money every month.

As a Silver sponsor you get:
- Your name in the jerrycan README (Sponsors section)
- The GitHub sponsor badge on your profile
- My genuine gratitude, which scales better than most backends

## Tier 2: Gold - $250 / month

**Name:** 🥇 Gold Sponsor

**Description:**

You fund a full deploy target. New platforms (after Render: Fly, Hetzner, Railway) each need building, hardening and permanent eval coverage.

As a Gold sponsor you get:
- Your logo in the jerrycan README (Sponsors section)
- Priority triage on your issues: reported bugs and doc gaps go to the top of the queue
- Everything from Silver

## Tier 3: Diamond - $500 / month

**Name:** 💎 Diamond Sponsor

**Description:**

You directly fund development time on humanity's last backend framework.

As a Diamond sponsor you get:
- Your logo at the top of the jerrycan README and on jerrycan.cc
- Priority triage on your issues
- A monthly 1:1 call: I look at your agent-backend setup, your design.json, your generated code, and help you get the most out of jerrycan
- Everything from Gold and Silver

---

## Hinweise

- Bei der Einrichtung fragt GitHub nach einer "featured description" für die Sponsor-Seite. Kurzfassung: "I build jerrycan, the AI-native Rust backend framework: agents design, generate, verify and deploy complete backends. Sponsorship pays for eval infrastructure, deploy targets, and development time."
- Custom amounts aktivieren (one-time + monthly), Minimum $5, damit kleine Unterstützer nicht wegfallen.
- Sobald das Listing live ist: der Sponsors-Button erscheint automatisch am Repo (FUNDING.yml zeigt auf Sorcecoder + Buy Me a Coffee).
